Small browser games, playable right here.

## [Tetris](/games/tetris/)

The classic falling-blocks game, with hold, ghost piece, next-piece preview and a high score saved in your browser. Use the keyboard on desktop or the on-screen buttons on mobile.

## [Tetris vs. AI](/games/tetris-vs-ai/)

Race an AI on identical piece sequences. Clear 2, 3 or 4 lines at once to send garbage rows to its board; last one standing wins. The AI scores every possible placement by stack height, holes, bumpiness and lines cleared, and comes in four difficulties, from Easy to Insane.
